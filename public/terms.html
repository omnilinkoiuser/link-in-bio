OmniLink rules

By publishing a page, you agree to these rules.

Don't publish

  • Scams, fake login pages, or anything that tricks people out of money or information
  • Pages pretending to be another person or business
  • Illegal content, or content you don't have the rights to use
  • Harassment, hate, or sexual content involving minors
  • Viruses or links to harmful software

Your page

You own what you publish and are responsible for it. Your page and everything on it are public. You can take it offline at any time from the publish screen.

What we can do

We can remove any page that breaks these rules. OmniLink is provided as is, and we can't promise it will always be available, so keep your own copy of anything important.

Report a page

Open a report with the page's address and what's wrong. Reports are public, so don't include private information.
